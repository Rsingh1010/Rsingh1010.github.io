Airbnb Pricing Analysis: NYC vs. Chicago

Comparing short-term rental pricing across two markets, and being honest about how little the obvious features explain.

  • Cleaned and analyzed Airbnb listings across NYC and Chicago, building price-distribution and neighborhood-level heatmap visualizations.
  • Found NYC priced modestly higher than Chicago ($106 vs. $99 median), with premium listings concentrated in Manhattan and Brooklyn versus Chicago's Loop and Near North Side.
  • Built linear regression and KMeans models to predict price from listing features; both showed low explanatory power (R² of 0.13 and 0.10), a clean result showing surface-level features alone don't explain Airbnb pricing.
